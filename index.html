How to Use
Conference Room

Step-by-step guide for Google Meet — no account needed, nothing to install.

🖥️ Intel NUC — Main PC
📷 Logitech MeetUp — Camera
🔊 Jabra Speak 750 — Mic & Speaker
📺 LG Smart TV 75" — Display
Start here
Start a Google Meet
Follow these steps in order. Click each step to expand details.
1
Turn on the TV and PC
Make sure both devices are on before starting
▾
Press the power button on the LG TV using the remote. Wait until the screen is fully on.
If the TV shows a black screen, press INPUT on the remote and select HDMI 1 (or whichever port the NUC is connected to).
The Intel NUC is usually already on. If not, press the small power button on the NUC unit.
⚡ The guide wallpaper on the TV screen means the PC is on and ready to use.
2
Open Google Meet in the browser
There's already a shortcut on the PC desktop
▾
Use the mouse and keyboard on the table.
Double-click the Google Meet icon on the desktop, or open Chrome and type meet.google.com in the address bar.
Google Meet will open directly — no login required.
3
Enter the meeting code
The code is sent by the meeting organiser
▾

Find the meeting code in your email invite or message from the organiser. Example format: abc-defg-hij

Click the "Enter a code or link" field on meet.google.com.
Type or paste the meeting code, then press Enter or click Join.
You will enter a waiting room. Click Ask to join or Join now.
💡 If the organiser shares a link instead of a code, just tap it on your phone — the browser will open automatically.
4
Check camera & audio are working
Check before joining the meeting
▾

Before joining, Google Meet shows a camera preview. Make sure:

Camera is active — you should see a room preview on screen. If not, check the troubleshooting section below.
Microphone is not muted — the mic icon should not have a red line through it.
Click the ⋮ (three dots) icon in the preview to manually select audio/video devices if needed.
5
When the meeting ends
Shut down properly so it's ready for the next session
▾
Click the red ☎ Leave call button at the bottom of the Meet screen.
Close Chrome.
Turn off the TV with the remote. The PC can be left on or shut down per office policy.
🔒 Since no Google account is signed in on this PC, no meeting data is stored after the browser is closed.
Audio Settings
Make sure Jabra is selected as the main audio device
This is important so audio goes through the Jabra Speak, not the MeetUp camera.
Settings in Google Meet Check this on first use
How to open
Click ⋮ → Settings → Audio Inside a meeting, click the three-dot icon at the bottom right, choose Settings, then the Audio tab.
Microphone
Jabra Speak 750 Stereo (or Jabra) Do not select "Logitech MeetUp" as the microphone.
Speaker
Jabra Speak 750 Stereo (or Jabra) Participant audio will come through the Jabra, not the TV.
Camera
Logitech MeetUp Only the camera uses the MeetUp.
Help
Frequently asked questions
Check here first before contacting IT support.
? TV screen is black, no image from the PC
Press the INPUT button on the TV remote and select HDMI 1 (or try other HDMI ports until the image appears). Make sure the HDMI cable between the NUC and TV is properly connected.
? Camera not detected in Google Meet
If a camera permission popup appears in the browser, click Allow. If it still does not show, check that the Logitech MeetUp USB cable is connected to the PC. Try refreshing the browser page.
? No audio from speakers / others cannot hear me
Go to Audio Settings in Google Meet (⋮ → Settings → Audio). Make sure both Microphone and Speaker are set to Jabra Speak 750. If Jabra does not appear, check the Jabra USB cable to the PC.
? Echo or reverb during the meeting
Echo usually happens when two audio devices are active at the same time. Make sure only the Jabra is selected as microphone and speaker in Google Meet — not the Logitech MeetUp and not the PC's built-in audio.
? Need further assistance
Contact the IT team via the ticketing system or the direct contact posted on the room information board.
