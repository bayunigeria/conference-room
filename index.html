How it works
Start a meeting in 4 steps
Follow the steps below.
1
Turn on the screen
Turn on the TV using the remote. The conference room launcher should appear on screen automatically.

If the screen is black, press INPUT on the remote and select HDMI 1.
2
Enter your meeting code on screen
The screen shows the Conference Room launcher. Using the keyboard on the table, click the Meeting code input field and type the code from your invite.

Example format: abc-defg-hij

You can also paste a full Google Meet link — it will extract the code automatically.
💡 No Google account needed. The launcher opens Meet directly without any login.
3
Click Join Meeting
Press Enter or click the green Open Meeting button. Google Meet will open in a new browser window automatically.

You will land in a waiting room — click Ask to join or Join now to enter. The launcher screen stays open in the background.
📹 Before joining, confirm the camera preview shows the room. If not, see the troubleshooting section below.
4
When the meeting ends
Click the red ☎ Leave call button, then close the Google Meet browser window.

The conference room launcher will still be on screen — ready for the next meeting.
🔒 No meeting data is stored — no Google account is signed in on this computer.
Audio Setup
Make sure Jabra is the default audio device
Do this once when first using the room — settings are saved by Chrome.
Audio settings in Google Meet Check on first use
How to open
Click ⋮ → Settings → Audio Inside a meeting, click the three-dot icon ⋮ at the bottom right, choose Settings, then the Audio tab.
Microphone
Jabra Speak 750 Stereo Do not select Logitech MeetUp as the microphone.
Speaker
Jabra Speak 750 Stereo Audio from remote participants will play through the Jabra units, not the TV.
Camera
Logitech MeetUp Only the camera uses the MeetUp — audio always uses Jabra.
Help
Troubleshooting
Check here first before contacting IT support.
? The TV screen is black
Press INPUT on the TV remote and select HDMI 1. If the screen is still black, contact IT support.
? The conference room launcher screen is not showing
The PC may not be on. Ask IT support to check the computer. The launcher screen should appear on the TV once the PC is running.
? Camera not detected in Google Meet
If a camera permission popup appears in the browser, click Allow. If no preview shows, try refreshing the page with F5. If the problem continues, contact IT support.
? No audio — others can't hear me or I can't hear them
Go to ⋮ → Settings → Audio inside the meeting. Set both Microphone and Speaker to Jabra Speak 750. If Jabra is not listed, contact IT support to check the audio connection.
? Echo or reverb during the call
This happens when two audio devices are active at the same time. Make sure only Jabra Speak 750 is selected for both Microphone and Speaker in Google Meet settings — not Logitech MeetUp and not the PC's built-in audio.
? The Open Meeting button is not working
Make sure the meeting code is entered correctly — example format: abc-defg-hij. The code is case-insensitive. You can also paste the full Google Meet URL into the input field.
? Need further help
Contact the IT team via the ticketing system or the direct contact posted on the room information board.
